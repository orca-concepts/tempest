By using its Service, you (“You” or “User”) are agreeing to the following, whether or not you are a registered customer or User of Orca (orcaconcepts.org). This Privacy Policy (“Privacy Policy”) may be updated from time to time without notice to you. Capitalized terms or other terms not herein defined are defined in the Subscriber Agreement and Terms of Use (“Agreement”) (orcaconcepts.org/terms). This Privacy Policy applies to personal data collected in connection with the Company Website(s) and the Website’s processing activities, as reflected in the Agreement.

  1. DATA WE COLLECT.
  1. Account data (collected at registration):
  1. Username (chosen by User)
  2. Email address (used for verification, legal notifications, copyright violation notices, and other policy updates); it is not publicly visible
  3. Password (stored as a one-way hash; used for login)
  4. ORCID ID (verified via OAuth with orcid.org; required to register)
  5. Records of the User’s agreement to the Agreement (date and version accepted) and of the User’s confirmation that they are 18 or older (date)
  1. How Passwords are Stored
  1. Passwords are hashed using bcryptjs (10 salt rounds). The original plain-text password is NEVER stored.

  1. DATA WE DO NOT COLLECT
  1. Location data
  2. IP addresses (beyond what is inherent in server logs)
  3. Tracking cookies or analytics data
  4. Browser fingerprints
  5. Third-party advertising or analytics identifiers
  6. Data from anyone under age 18

  1. USER ACTIVITY DATA

User activity on the Website varies in its visibility:

  1. Attributed by username: Links added to questions, page comments, moderation comments, addenda added to links, and reader feedback and replies posted in curated communities
  2. Visible but not attributed: Questions and edges created in the graph hierarchy, votes (graph votes, swap votes, link votes, and votes on reader feedback). These are stored with the User's ID internally but the username is not displayed in the user interface (“UI”).
  3. Communities and curation: the User who creates a community is recorded as its creator, and each curated community records its owners. Answers that owners add, and questions they remove, are stored with the owner’s User ID. A curated community’s owners are shown by username to its other owners.

Users with verified ORCID IDs will have a small ORCID icon displayed alongside their username wherever it appears in authorship or membership contexts.

  1. DATA WE SHARE AND DO NOT SHARE
  1. We do not sell, rent, or share User data with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.
  2. ORCID verification: During the ORCID linking process, the User is redirected to orcid.org to authenticate. Orca receives the verified ORCID ID, the name on the ORCID record, and the email addresses on the ORCID record that are visible to Orca (so the User can choose an already-verified email). Only the ORCID ID and the email the User chooses are stored — no access tokens are stored. This is subject to ORCID's privacy policy (https://info.orcid.org/privacy-policy/ ).
  3. As an open-source platform, the source code is publicly available, but the DATABASE (containing User data and Content) is not.

  1. COOKIES AND LOCAL STORAGE
  1. Orca uses a JWT (JSON Web Token) stored in the browser for authentication session management. Local storage is used for UI preferences (e.g., the community the User last viewed).
  2. No third-party cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics scripts are used.

  1. DATA RETENTION
  1. Account data (username, email, hashed password, ORCID iD) is retained until the User deletes their account.
  2. User-generated Content follows the four-tier policy described in the Agreement:
  1. Questions and edges are retained permanently (creator field cleared on account deletion where applicable). In curated communities, owners may remove questions; removed questions are hidden from view but retained in the database.
  2. Votes are deleted when the account is deleted.
  3. Links can be removed by the user that posted them. In curated communities, the community’s owners can also edit and remove answers (links).
  4. Reader feedback and replies in curated communities are retained. Owners may hide them, and feedback on a removed question is archived (kept, read-only). When an account is deleted, the author’s username is removed from that User’s feedback and replies.
  5. Page comments and moderation comments are deleted when the account is deleted.
  1. Dormant tables and retired features: Some database tables are retained per the platform's append-only philosophy even after the associated feature is retired. This data is inert and not actively processed.

  1. DATA SECURITY
  1. Passwords are hashed using bcryptjs (10 salt rounds) and validated for strength using the zxcvbn library.
  2. Authentication tokens (JWTs) can be invalidated globally via "Log out everywhere" feature.
  3. The Website(s) uses rate limiting on authentication endpoints (10 login attempts per IP per 15 minutes; 5 password-reset requests per IP per hour), plus per-User limits on creating content and an overall limit on requests per IP.
  4. ORCID integration uses server-to-server OAuth token exchange — ORCID access tokens are used once for verification and not stored.
  5. We take other industry standard security measures to protect data.

  1. USER RIGHTS
  1. Access: Users can view their contributions through the Website interface: voted Questions are visible via graph votes and voted links are visible via link votes. Users can view their profile page showing their username, ORCID ID (if linked). There is not currently a single unified view of all contributions.
  2. Deletion: Users can delete their account, which removes personal data and clears creator attribution on contributions (see Section 16 of the Agreement). Users can also delete links they posted (see Section 5 of the Agreement). A User who is the only owner of a curated community must add another owner before the account can be deleted.
  3. Our data export feature may be found on your profile page (click your username at the top and look under Privacy & Data). Please contact us at orcaconcepts@gmail.com if you believe your personal data needs correction or you need assistance with data export.

  1. THIRD PARTY SERVICES
  1. ORCID: Used for User (researcher) identity verification via OAuth. The verified ORCID ID, the name on the ORCID record, and the email addresses visible to Orca are received; only the ORCID ID (and the email the User chooses) is stored; no access tokens are retained. This is subject to ORCID's privacy policy (https://info.orcid.org/privacy-policy/ ).
  2. Railway: Used for application hosting (web server and database). This is subject to Railway's privacy policy (https://railway.com/legal/privacy ).
  3. Resend: Used to send transactional emails to Users (email address verification, password reset, and welcome emails). The User’s email address is transmitted to Resend solely for the purpose of delivering these emails. Resend acts as a data processor on our behalf and is subject to Resend’s privacy policy (https://resend.com/legal/privacy-policy ).
  4. Cloudflare: Used for DNS resolution, content delivery (CDN), and traffic security (DDoS protection, bot mitigation). As traffic to orcaconcepts.org passes through Cloudflare’s network, Cloudflare receives standard request data including User IP addresses, browser/device information, and the URLs requested. Cloudflare acts as a data processor on our behalf and is subject to Cloudflare’s privacy policy (https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/ ).
  5. Google Safe Browsing: When a User adds a link, the link’s web address is sent to Google’s Safe Browsing service to check it against lists of malware and phishing sites. Only the web address is sent, not the User’s identity. Links that fail the check are rejected, and the attempt is logged with the submitting User’s ID. This is subject to Google’s privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy ).
  6. No third-party services are used for advertising, analytics, or tracking.
